# WorkhavenHR
WorkhavenHR is an all-in-one HR SaaS platform built to streamline your people operations from hire to retire. Eliminate messy spreadsheets, automate payroll and leave tracking, and empower your team with self-service tools, all in one secure, cloud-based workspace.

## Core Capabilities

* **Employee Lifecycle Management:** Centralized directory, role-based access control (RBAC), digital document storage, and automated onboarding/offboarding checklists.
* **Automated Payroll Engine:** Configurable pay cycles, automated tax and statutory deduction calculations, and one-click payslip generation.
* **Time & Leave Tracking:** Customizable leave policies, shift scheduling, and manager approval workflows.
* **Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal:** Empowers team members to update personal records, request time off, view org charts, and download tax or payroll documents independently.
* **People Analytics & Reporting:** Exportable audit logs and real-time dashboards tracking headcount, retention, and attendance trends.
